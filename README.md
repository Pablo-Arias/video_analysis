# Introduction
A wraper around cool python libraries to do face analysis.

## Modules
- `face_analysis_mp.py`: MediaPipe face landmarks and blendshapes (tutorial: `tutorials/face_analysis_tutorial.md`)
- `head_movement_analysis.py`: head pose, iris gaze and eye aspect ratio; measures: head angular speed, wavelet spectrogram, fast nod score (Falk et al. 2023), slow nod coherence (Hale et al. 2020) (tutorial: `tutorials/head_movement_tutorial.ipynb`)
- `gesture_analysis.py`: hands, upper-body pose and motion energy (tutorial: `tutorials/gesture_analysis_tutorial.md`)
- `face_analysis_pf.py`, `hr_analysis.py`, `transcribe.py`
